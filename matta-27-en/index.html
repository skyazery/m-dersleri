The Greatest Commandment

Matthew 21:23–22:46

Tap the yellow words to see what they mean. The 🔊 button reads aloud.

What will we learn today?

  • We will learn to obey with actions rather than just words.
  • We will learn that God invites everyone to His banquet.
  • We will learn that loving God and our neighbor is the greatest commandment.
Scene 1 · Matthew 21:28–32

The Two Sons

A father said to his two sons, 'Go and work in the vineyard today.' The older son said, 'I don't want to,' but later he changed his mind and went to work.

The younger son said, 'Yes, I will go!' but he didn't go. Jesus asked, 'Which one did what his father wanted?' Actions that show are more important than just words.

Scene 2 · Matthew 22:1–10

The King's Wedding Banquet

A king prepared a for his son and called the guests. But the people who were invited went to their fields and businesses and didn't come.

The king told his servants, 'Go out to the roads and invite anyone you see to the banquet.' So the wedding hall was filled with guests. God everyone.

Scene 3 · Matthew 22:15–22

Caesar's to Caesar

People asked Jesus a question to trick Him: 'Is it right to pay to the Roman emperor or not?'

Jesus looked at a coin and asked, 'Whose face is on this?' 'The emperor's.' 'Then give to Caesar what belongs to Caesar, and give to God what belongs to God.' The people were amazed.

Scene 4 · Matthew 22:34–40

The Greatest Commandment

A teacher of the law asked, 'Which is the greatest?'

Jesus answered, 'Love the Lord your God with all your heart, with all your soul, and with all your mind. This is the first commandment. The second is like it: Love your as yourself.'
