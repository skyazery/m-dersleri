What will we learn today?

  • We will learn stories of Jesus healing sick people.
  • We will learn that there is great power in Jesus' words.
  • We will learn to trust Jesus when we are afraid.
Scene 1 · Matthew 8:1–4

He touched the man with leprosy

A man with came to Jesus and bowed down: “Lord, if you want to, you can make me clean.”

No one touched people with leprosy. But Jesus reached out His hand and touched him: “I want to. Be clean!” Instantly the man was healed.
