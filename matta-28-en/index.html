Bible lesson for children · Matthew, lesson 28

Stay Awake

Matthew 23:1–24:51

Tap the yellow words to see what they mean. The 🔊 button reads aloud.

What will we learn today?

  • We will learn to be people who humble themselves, not exalt themselves.
  • We will learn that Jesus wants to gather us under His wings like a hen.
  • We will learn to stay awake and ready, waiting for the day Jesus comes again.
Scene 1 · Matthew 23:1–12

Those Who Love the Highest Places

The spoke well, but they didn't do what they said. They dressed up their clothes to be seen by people, and they wanted to sit in the highest places at feasts.

Jesus said, “He who is greatest among you will be your servant. Whoever exalts himself will be humbled, and whoever himself will be exalted.”

Scene 2 · Matthew 23:37–39

Like a Hen Gathering Her Chicks

Jesus looked at Jerusalem and was sad: “O Jerusalem, Jerusalem!”

“How often I wanted to gather your children together, just like a gathers her chicks under her wings, but you didn't want to.” Jesus wants to hold us in His warm embrace.

Scene 3 · Matthew 24:1–14

The Temple That Will Fall

The disciples were amazed by the big and beautiful temple building. Jesus said, “There will not be left here one stone on another, that will not be thrown down.”

Jesus said there would be wars, earthquakes, and hard times in the future. But “he who to the end will be saved. This Gospel will be preached in the whole world.”

Scene 4 · Matthew 24:36–51

The Watchful Servant

Jesus will come again. But no one knows the day and hour. That is why Jesus said, “Be !”

Blessed is that who faithfully does his given work, no matter when his master comes. We too live well every day, waiting for Jesus.
