Bible lesson for teens · Matthew, lesson 9

Prayer to the Father

Matthew 6:1–18

Likes, followers, view counts. We are used to 'being seen'. Speaking of charity, prayer, and fasting—the three pillars of faith—Jesus asked the same question: who do you want to be seen by?

Tap the underlined words: meaning, original-language form and Azerbaijani word.

Background

We understand the danger of 'faith to be seen' in charity, prayer, and fasting.

Main idea

We can explain the structure of the Lord's Prayer and the meaning of each request.

Application

We pray the Lord's Prayer to God in our own words.

1 · Matthew 6:1–4

To be seen by whom?

“Be careful that you don't do your charitable giving before men, to be seen by them.” Jesus says that blow trumpets when they give .

“They have received their reward in full.” That means people's praise is all the reward they will get.

Look Deeper · Three pillars

Charity, prayer, and fasting were the three pillars of Jewish piety. Jesus did not abolish them. At the end of all three comes the same refrain: “Your Father who sees in secret will reward you.”

2 · Matthew 6:5–8

The room and the Father

In those days, houses had a small storage for grain and tools, without even a window. Jesus says to go in there, close the door, and pray.

Do not think you will be heard for your many words like the pagans. Because God is a 'Father' who already knows.

Look Deeper · 'Father'

In the Sermon on the Mount, the word 'Father' appears 17 times, 10 of which are in 6:1–18. Prayer is not transmitting information, but a relationship with the Father.

3 · Matthew 6:9–13

The Lord's Prayer

“Pray like this.” The first three requests of the Lord's Prayer ask for the 'Father's' name, Kingdom, and will, while the last three ask for 'our' bread, debts, and temptation.

God's work comes first, my needs come second. But my needs are not forgotten. The Father cares about both.

Look Deeper · 'We'

The word 'I' does not appear even once in the Lord's Prayer. 'Our Father', 'our bread', 'our debts'. Even if we pray alone in our room, we pray together with God's whole family.

4 · Matthew 6:14–18

Forgiveness and fasting

Jesus explains only one request from the Lord's Prayer again: . “If you don't forgive men their trespasses, neither will your Father forgive your trespasses.”

When you fast, do not look gloomy, but anoint your head and wash your face. Faith is not a performance.

Look Deeper · Is forgiveness a condition?

This does not mean 'buying' forgiveness. A forgiven person becomes a forgiving person. If we reject forgiving to the end, we need to examine whether we have truly accepted God's forgiveness (18:21–35).

My Lord's Prayer

Closing prayer

Our Father in heaven, help me to be free from a faith meant to be seen, and to live sincerely before You. May I seek Your name, Your Kingdom, and Your will before my own needs. As a forgiven person, let me also forgive. I pray in the name of Jesus Christ. Amen.
