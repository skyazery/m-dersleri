Bible lesson for teens · Matthew, lesson 2

Who is the True King?

Matthew 2:1–12

Hearing the same news, some travel a long way to kneel, while others draw their swords. What is your response before Jesus?

Background

We compare the different responses of the wise men, Herod, and the religious leaders.

Main idea

We can explain that Jesus is the King of the whole world, not just the Jews.

Application

We look at who sits on the 'throne' of our lives.

1 · Matthew 2:1–2

Gentiles Following the Star

The were scholars from the area of modern-day Iran and Iraq. They studied stars and dreams. They were not Jews, but .

Surprisingly, the first to seek and find the Messiah were not the Jews who knew the Bible well, but Gentiles from a faraway country. Just as Matthew included Gentile women in the genealogy in the first chapter, here too he shows that Jesus is the King of all nations.

A Deeper Look · The Star and Prophecy

Numbers 24:17 says, “A star will come out of Jacob.” God guided the men who studied stars in a way they could understand, and finally led them to Jesus through His Word.

2 · Matthew 2:3–8

A Fearful King, Unmoving Scholars

King was a king appointed by Rome. He was so suspicious that he would even kill his own family to protect his throne. The birth of the “King of the Jews” was a threat to him.

The and teachers of the law whom Herod gathered knew the word of the prophet well Mic. 5:2: the Messiah would be born in Bethlehem. But not one of them went to Bethlehem, which was only 9 kilometers away.

A Deeper Look · Knowing and Going

Scholars who knew the Word but didn't move; wise men who knew little but traveled a long way. Faith doesn't end with knowing; it continues with steps taken toward Jesus.

3 · Matthew 2:9–11

Three Gifts

When the star stopped over the house where the baby was, the wise men rejoiced with exceedingly great joy. They bowed down, , and gave gifts of gold, , and .

For a long time, these gifts have been explained as signs showing who Jesus is.

A Deeper Look · The Meaning of the Gifts

Gold was given to kings, frankincense was burned in worship to God, and myrrh was used in burials. Therefore, much of Christian tradition explains these gifts like this: Jesus the King, Jesus who is God, and Jesus who would die for us.

4 · Matthew 2:12

Another Way

God warned the wise men in a dream not to return to Herod. They went back to their own country by another way.

A person who meets Jesus does not go back the same way. After this meeting, the direction of life changes.

Discussion questions

  1. Like Herod, have I ever pushed Jesus away out of fear of losing my position?
  2. What is the difference between 'knowing' the Word and 'going' to Jesus?
  3. What do I give to Jesus? Choose one from time, skills, and heart, and talk about it.
