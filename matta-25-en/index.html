Bible lesson for children · Matthew, lesson 25

Jesus, Who Came to Serve

Matthew 20:1–34

Tap the yellow words to see what they mean. The 🔊 button reads aloud.

What will we learn today?

  • We will learn about God's generous grace through the parable of the vineyard owner.
  • We will learn that the one who serves is great in the kingdom of heaven.
  • We will decide to serve others like Jesus.
Scene 1 · Matthew 20:1–7

The workers in the vineyard

A owner went to the marketplace early in the morning and called workers. The daily wage was one denarius.

The owner went out at nine o'clock, twelve o'clock, three o'clock, and even at five o'clock, one hour before sunset, and called people standing around without work.

Scene 2 · Matthew 20:8–16

Everyone got the same

In the evening, the owner gave the wages, starting from the last ones to arrive. Even the one who worked for one hour got a ! Those who worked since morning thought they would get more, but they also got one denarius.

When they grumbled, the owner said: “I kept my word. Are you upset because I am ?” God is equally generous to those who come late.

Scene 3 · Matthew 20:20–28

Give the highest place

The mother of James and John came and asked: “Let my two sons sit in Your Kingdom, one on Your right and one on Your left.” The other disciples were angry.

Jesus said: “Whoever wants to be great among you must be a to others. I also did not come to be served, but to serve and to give my life for many.”

Scene 4 · Matthew 20:29–34

Two men whose eyes were opened

Two men sitting by the road in Jericho shouted: “Lord, Son of David, have mercy on us!” Even though the people told them to be quiet, they shouted even louder.

Jesus stopped and asked: “What do you want me to do for you?” “Let our eyes be opened.” Jesus had compassion, touched their eyes, and immediately they saw and followed Him.
