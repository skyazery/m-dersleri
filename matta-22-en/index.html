Bible lesson for children · Matthew, lesson 22

Listen to Him

Matthew 17:1–27

Tap the yellow words to see what they mean. The 🔊 button reads aloud.

What will we learn today?

  • We will learn that Jesus' appearance changed with light on a high mountain.
  • We will learn that God said, 'Listen to Jesus.'
  • We will learn that even faith as small as a mustard seed is precious.
Scene 1 · Matthew 17:1–5

Jesus Shining Like the Sun

Jesus took Peter, James, and John up a high mountain. At that moment, Jesus' face shone like the sun, and His clothes became as white as light. He was .

Moses and appeared and were talking with Jesus. Then God's voice came from a bright cloud: 'This is my beloved Son, with whom I am well pleased. Listen to Him!'

Scene 2 · Matthew 17:6–13

Get Up, Don't Be Afraid

The disciples were very afraid and fell to the ground. Jesus came to them, them, and said, 'Get up, and don't be afraid.'

When the disciples looked up, they saw only Jesus. God is not scary; He comes close to us through Jesus.

Scene 3 · Matthew 17:14–21

He Healed the Sick Boy

At the bottom of the mountain, a father knelt before Jesus: 'Lord, have mercy on my son. He often falls into the fire and into the water.' The disciples could not heal him.

Jesus healed the boy. Then He said, 'If you have faith as small as a , you will say to this mountain, "Move from here to there," and it will move.'

Scene 4 · Matthew 17:24–27

The Coin in the Fish's Mouth

The people who collected the temple asked Peter, 'Doesn't your teacher pay the tax?'

Jesus said, 'Go to the lake and throw in your line. Open the mouth of the first fish you catch, and you will find a coin. Take it and give it to them for Me and you.' Peter really found the coin!
