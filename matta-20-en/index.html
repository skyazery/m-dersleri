Woman, you have great faith

Matthew 15:1–39

Tap the yellow words to see what they mean. The 🔊 button reads aloud.

What will we learn today?

  • We will learn that God looks at the heart more than the outside.
  • We will learn about the great faith of the Canaanite woman.
  • We will learn to pray to Jesus without giving up.
Scene 1 · Matthew 15:1–9

They didn't wash their hands?

The Pharisees asked, “Why don't your disciples wash their hands before eating according to the ?”

Jesus said, “You honor God with your lips, but your heart is far from Him.” God looks at the more than the outside.

Scene 2 · Matthew 15:10–20

What comes out of the heart

“What goes into the mouth does not make a person unclean. What comes out of the mouth makes a person unclean.”

Bad thoughts, lies, and hateful words come out of the heart. That is why our hearts must be clean.

Scene 3 · Matthew 15:21–28

A mother who didn't give up

A woman living in the land of the Gentiles cried out, “Lord, Son of David, have mercy on me! My daughter is suffering terribly.”

At first, Jesus did not answer, but the woman did not give up. “Even the dogs eat the that fall from their masters' table.” Jesus said, “Woman, you have great ! Let it be as you wish.” At that moment, her daughter was healed.

Scene 4 · Matthew 15:29–39

Feeding four thousand people

When Jesus sat on the mountain, people brought the lame, the blind, and the mute to Him. Jesus healed them all.

Jesus had compassion on the crowd who had stayed with Him for three days. He fed four thousand people with seven loaves of bread and a few small fish. Seven baskets were left over.
