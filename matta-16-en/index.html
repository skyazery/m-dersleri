Lord of the Sabbath

Matthew 12:1–50

What will we learn today?

  • We will learn that the Sabbath is a wonderful gift for people.
  • We will learn that Jesus healed a shriveled hand on the Sabbath.
  • We will learn that those who do God's will are Jesus's family.
Scene 1 · Matthew 12:1–8

Through the grain fields

One Jesus and His disciples were walking through the grain fields. His hungry disciples began to pick heads of grain and eat them.

The Pharisees got angry and said, “You can't work on the Sabbath!” Jesus said, “God wants , not . I am the Lord of the Sabbath.”

Scene 2 · Matthew 12:9–14

The man with a shriveled hand

In the synagogue, there was a man with a shriveled hand that couldn't move. The people watched to see if Jesus would heal on the Sabbath.

Jesus said, “If your sheep falls into a pit on the Sabbath, wouldn't you lift it out? A person is much more valuable than a sheep.” “Stretch out your hand!” The man's hand was completely healed.

Scene 3 · Matthew 12:15–21

He won't break a bruised reed

Jesus didn't shout loudly or argue. He quietly healed the sick.

The Bible says about Jesus: “He won't break a bruised , and He won't put out a smoldering wick.” He cares for weak people with kindness.

My family

Jesus's mother and brothers came to see Him. Jesus pointed to His disciples and said:
