Come to Me, All of You

Matthew 11:1–30

What will we learn today?

  • We will learn how Jesus answered John the Baptist's question from prison.
  • We will learn that God reveals His Kingdom to little children.
  • We will learn to go to Jesus and find rest when we are tired.
Scene 1 · Matthew 11:1–6

A Question from Prison

John the Baptist was put in by King Herod. John sent his disciples to ask: “Are You the one who is to come?”

Jesus answered: “Go and tell what you hear and see: the blind see, the lame walk, and the is preached to the poor.” Jesus is the one who was to come.

Scene 2 · Matthew 11:16–19

We Played the Flute, But...

Jesus talked about children in the market: “We played the for you, and you didn't dance. We sang a sad song, and you didn't cry.”

The people didn't like John, and they didn't like Jesus either. No matter what happened, they didn't open their hearts.

Scene 3 · Matthew 11:25–27

Revealed to Little Children

Jesus prayed: “Father, I thank You that You have hidden these things from the wise and smart, and revealed them to little children.”

God's Kingdom is understood not by smart people, but by those who open their hearts like a child. This is greater than .

Scene 4 · Matthew 11:28–30

Come to Me and Rest

Jesus said: “Come to Me, all you who are tired and carry heavy , and I will give you rest.”

“I am and humble in heart. My is easy, and my burden is light.” When we are tired, we find peace by going to Jesus.
