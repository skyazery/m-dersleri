The salt and light of the world

Matthew 5:1–16

Tap the yellow words to see what they mean. The 🔊 button reads aloud.

What will we learn today?

  • We will learn the words of 'blessing' Jesus taught on the mountain.
  • We will learn who God blesses.
  • We will find out how we can live as the salt and light of the world.
Scene 1 · Matthew 5:1–2

The Teacher on the Mountain

Many people followed Jesus. Jesus went up on a and sat down, and His disciples and the people sat around Him.

Jesus began to speak and teach. These words are called the Sermon on the Mount.

Scene 2 · Matthew 5:3–12

Such people are blessed

Jesus said amazing words: “ are the poor in spirit. Blessed are those who mourn, for God will comfort them.”

“Blessed are the , the merciful, the pure in heart, and the .” God considers weak and humble people very precious.

Scene 3 · Matthew 5:13

You are the salt of the world

Jesus said: “You are the of the world.”

Salt makes food tasty and keeps it from spoiling. A person who follows Jesus is someone who brings a good flavor among their friends.

Scene 4 · Matthew 5:14–16

You are the light of the world

“You are the of the world. People do not light a lamp and put it under a bowl, but they put it on a so it gives light to everyone in the house.”

When we do good deeds, people see them and God. We are little lamps shining Jesus' light.
