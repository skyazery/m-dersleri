Baby Jesus Protected by God

What will we learn today?

  • We will learn that Joseph's family fled to Egypt.
  • We will learn that God protects us even in times of danger.
  • We will learn to obey God's word immediately, just like Joseph.

A Long and Hard Journey

Joseph's family rode a donkey across the desert to to Egypt. It was a very long and hard journey.

They lived in Egypt until Herod died. This made the words of the come true: “I called my son out of Egypt.”

Herod's Anger

When the wise men didn't come back, Herod got very angry. He gave a very bad order to hurt the little children in Bethlehem.

The mothers in Bethlehem cried a lot. God sees children's tears and is sad with them. God also baby Jesus.
