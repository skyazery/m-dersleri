What will we learn today?

  • We will learn that Jesus prayed in the Garden of Gethsemane.
  • We will learn that Jesus being arrested and judged was for us.
  • We will learn about tears of repentance through Peter who stumbled.
Scene 1 · Matthew 26:36–46

Prayer in Gethsemane

Jesus went to the Garden of and prayed with a very sad and troubled heart: “Father, if it is possible, let this cup pass away from me.”

Then he continued: “Nevertheless, not as I will, but as you will.” But the disciples could not stay awake and fell asleep.
