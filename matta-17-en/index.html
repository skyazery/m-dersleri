Bible lesson for children · Matthew, lesson 17

The Farmer Who Planted Seeds

Matthew 13:1–23

Tap the yellow words to see what they mean. The 🔊 button reads aloud.

What will we learn today?

  • We will learn the parable of the farmer planting seeds that Jesus told.
  • We will learn that the seed is the Word of God and the soil is our heart.
  • We will decide to be good soil that receives the Word well.
Scene 1 · Matthew 13:1–3

Jesus Teaching from a Boat

Because a large crowd gathered, Jesus got into a boat and sat down, while the people stood on the shore and listened.

Jesus taught many things using . A parable is a simple story that holds a secret about the kingdom of heaven.

Scene 2 · Matthew 13:4–6, 19–21

The Path and the Rocky Soil

Some fell along the path, and birds came and ate them. This is a heart that hears the Word but quickly forgets it.

Some fell on rocky soil where there wasn't much dirt. They sprouted quickly, but because they had no , they dried up when the sun came out. This is a heart that is happy at first but gives up quickly when trouble comes.

Scene 3 · Matthew 13:7, 22

The Thorny Soil

Some seeds fell among . The thorns grew up and choked the sprouts.

This is a heart that hears the Word, but because of too many worries and greed, the Word cannot grow.

Scene 4 · Matthew 13:8–9, 23

The Good Soil

But some seeds fell on . They grew and produced a —a hundred, sixty, or thirty times what was planted!

A heart that hears the Word, understands it, and obeys it is the good soil. Jesus said, “He who has ears, let him hear.”
