Bible lesson for children · Matthew, lesson 10

Do not worry

Matthew 6:19–34

Tap the yellow words to see what they mean. The 🔊 button reads aloud.

What will we learn today?

  • We will learn what it means to store up treasures in heaven.
  • We will learn that God cares for us just as He cares for the birds and flowers.
  • We will learn to seek God's Kingdom first instead of worrying.
Scene 1 · Matthew 6:19–21

Treasure stored in heaven

Jesus said, “Don't store up for yourselves on earth. Moths and rust destroy them there, and thieves break in and steal.”

“Store up treasures in heaven. For where your treasure is, there your heart will be also.” Loving and sharing is treasure stored in heaven.

Scene 2 · Matthew 6:22–24

Two masters

“No one can serve two .”

“You can't serve both God and wealth.” We need money, but the Master of our hearts is only God.

Scene 3 · Matthew 6:25–30

Look at the birds and flowers

“Don't about what you will eat or what you will wear. Look at the birds in the sky. They don't plant seeds, but God feeds them.”

“Look at the in the field. God dresses them more beautifully than King Solomon's clothes. How much more will He care for you!”

Scene 4 · Matthew 6:31–34

Seek first

“Your heavenly Father knows what you need.”

“Seek God's and His righteousness first, and all these things will be given to you as well. Don't worry about tomorrow.”

Memory verse of the week
