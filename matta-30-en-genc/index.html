Bible lesson for teens · Matthew, lesson 30

Perfume and Thirty Pieces of Silver

Matthew 26:1–35

On Wednesday and Thursday of Passion Week, sharply contrasting choices happen around Jesus. A woman pours out her most precious thing, while a disciple sells Jesus for the price of a slave. And that night, Jesus makes a new covenant with bread and a cup.

Background

Through the contrast between the woman who poured perfume and Judas, we look at our hearts toward Jesus.

Main idea

We can explain the meaning of the Passover meal turning into the Lord's Supper of the new covenant.

Application

We confess Jesus' unchanging love despite the disciples' weaknesses.

1 · Matthew 26:1–13

Beautiful Waste

The chief priests plotted to kill Jesus, saying, "Not during the feast, lest a riot occur among the people." Right then, in Bethany at the house of Simon the leper, a woman poured an alabaster jar of very expensive on Jesus' head.

The disciples were indignant, saying, "This could have been given to the poor." Jesus said, "She has done a good work for Me," and "She did it to prepare Me for burial." The woman was the first person to understand Jesus' death.

Looking Deeper · Anointing

"Christ" means "the anointed one." Oil was poured on the heads of kings. An unnamed woman, not a priest, anointed King Jesus. That King would take His throne on the cross.

2 · Matthew 26:14–16

The Price of a Slave

Immediately after this, Iscariot, "one of the twelve," went to the chief priests and asked, "What are you willing to give me?" They weighed out thirty pieces of silver for him.

Thirty pieces of silver was the compensation for a slave gored to death by an ox in {Exodus 21:32}. It also appears as the "wages of the shepherd" in {Zechariah 11:12–13}. While the woman poured perfume worth more than 300 denarii, Judas sold Jesus for a tenth of that price.

Looking Deeper · Why Judas?

The Bible does not detail Judas's motives. Money ({John 12:6}), disappointment regarding the Messiah, and Satan's influence ({Luke 22:3}) are mentioned. The important thing is this: even if you are with Jesus for three years, your heart can be somewhere else.

3 · Matthew 26:17–30

The Meal of the New Covenant

The meal was a meal to remember the exodus from Egypt. When Jesus said, "One of you will betray Me," the disciples asked one by one, "Lord, is it I?" But Judas said, "Rabbi, is it I?"

Jesus broke the bread and said, "Take, eat; this is My body." Then He gave the cup and said, "This is My blood of the , which is poured out for many for the forgiveness of sins." Jesus Himself stood in the place of the Passover lamb of the exodus.

Looking Deeper · "Lord" and "Rabbi"

The other disciples said "Lord" (kyrios), but Judas said "Rabbi." In the Gospel of Matthew, Judas is the only disciple who calls Jesus "Rabbi." Even though they sat at the same table, their confessions were different.

4 · Matthew 26:31–35

I Will Strike the Shepherd

While singing a hymn and going to the Mount of Olives, Jesus quoted {Zechariah 13:7}: "I will strike the shepherd, and the sheep of the flock will be scattered." At the same time, He made a promise: "After I am raised up, I will go before you into Galilee."

Peter said, "Even if everyone leaves You, I will never leave You," but Jesus said, "Tonight, before the rooster crows, you will Me three times." All the disciples said, "Even if we must die with You, we will not deny You," but that night they all fled.

Looking Deeper · A Promise Before Failure

Jesus gave the promise to meet again (in Galilee) "before" the disciples stumbled. Even knowing their failure, He does not cut off the relationship. In 28:7 and 10, this promise is fulfilled exactly.

5 questions

Discussion questions

  1. Have I ever given something unsparingly to Jesus, like the woman's "beautiful waste"?
  2. Like Judas, I might be close to Jesus, but my heart could be somewhere else. What are the "thirty pieces of silver" in my heart?
  3. What do I remember when I participate in the Lord's Supper?
  4. Have I ever been confident like Peter and then stumbled? What does Jesus' promise mean at that time?

My Confession

Closing prayer

Jesus, thank You for Your love in giving Your body and blood unsparingly for me, even though You were sold for thirty pieces of silver. Let me let go of the thirty pieces of silver in my heart and pour out my most precious thing like that woman. I trust in You, who holds me before I stumble, even when I am weak like Peter. I pray in the name of Jesus Christ. Amen.
