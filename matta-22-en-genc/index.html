Bible lesson for teens · Matthew, lesson 22

Glory on the Mountain, Reality at the Bottom

Matthew 17:1–27

Six days after announcing the cross, Jesus briefly lifts the curtain for three disciples to show them who He is. But when they come down from the mountain of glory, a demon-possessed boy and failing disciples are waiting. Faith is moving back and forth between these two places.

Background

To understand Jesus' identity and His relationship with the Law and the Prophets in the transfiguration event.

Main idea

To explain the failure at the bottom of the mountain and the meaning of 'mustard seed faith'.

Application

To determine how to live out the command 'Listen to Him' in daily life.

1 · Matthew 17:1–5

The Curtain is Lifted

Jesus was : His face shone like the sun, and His clothes became as white as light. Moses (the Law) and Elijah (the Prophets) appeared and talked with Jesus. This illustrates that the entire Old Testament points to Jesus.

Peter wanted to build three tents. At that moment, a bright cloud covered them, and a voice said: 'This is my beloved Son, with whom I am well pleased. Listen to Him!' One phrase was added to the voice from His baptism in 3:17: 'Listen.'

Look Deeper · Sinai and This Mountain

'After six days,' 'a high mountain,' 'a shining face,' 'a cloud,' and 'a voice' all recall the story of Moses in Exodus 24 and 34. Moses reflected God's glory, but Jesus is that glory Himself.

2 · Matthew 17:6–13

No One Except Jesus

The disciples were afraid and fell to the ground. Jesus came, them, and said, 'Get up, and don't be afraid.' When they looked up, they 'saw no one except Jesus.' Both Moses and Elijah disappeared, and only Jesus remained.

On the way down, the disciples asked about the prophecy that Elijah must come first. Jesus says that John the Baptist was that Elijah, and just as people did not recognize him, the Son of Man will also suffer.

Look Deeper · Why Tell No One?

Speaking only of glory before the resurrection would cause misunderstanding. People wanted a glorious Messiah without the cross. The glory on the mountain can only be fully understood by passing through the cross.

3 · Matthew 17:14–21

Failure at the Bottom of the Mountain

At the bottom of the mountain, there was a boy suffering from seizures and disciples who could not heal him. Even though they had received authority in 10:1, they failed. Jesus said, 'Faithless and perverse generation,' and healed the boy.

When the disciples asked why, He said, 'Because of your little faith,' and added, 'If you have faith as small as a , you will say to this mountain, "Move from here to there," and it will move.' The issue is not the size of the faith, but who the faith is directed toward.

Look Deeper · Little Faith and Mustard Seed Faith

'Little faith' (oligopistia) is a wavering faith mixed with doubt. But 'mustard seed faith,' even if small, is alive and relies completely on God. Just as there is life in a small seed, it is enough for faith to be genuine, even if it is small.

4 · Matthew 17:22–27

Sons Are Free

Jesus predicts His death and resurrection for the second time. The disciples were 'deeply grieved.' Then comes the story of the temple tax. Jewish men over twenty paid a half-shekel (two drachmas) every year.

Jesus says, 'The sons of the king do not pay taxes.' The Son of God, who is the Lord of the temple, does not need to pay. Yet, 'so as not to cause them to stumble,' He tells Peter to pay it and has him find a shekel in a fish's mouth. He chose love over His rights.

Look Deeper · So As Not to Cause Stumbling

Even if you have freedom, giving up that freedom so as not to cause others to stumble is the way of Jesus. Paul also speaks of the same principle (1 Cor. 8:13; Rom. 14:21).

5 questions

Discussion questions

  1. Have you ever felt the difference between a 'moment on the mountain' (a retreat, a grace-filled worship service) and the 'reality at the bottom of the mountain' (school, home)?
  2. God said, 'Listen to Him.' Whose voice do I 'listen to' the most these days?
  3. When does my faith waver like 'little faith'?
  4. In what area can I give up my freedom or rights for the sake of someone else?

Closing prayer

Dear God, thank You for showing that Jesus is Your beloved Son and the fulfillment of the Law and the Prophets. Amidst many voices, let me listen to the words of Jesus. Whether on the mountain or at the bottom of the mountain, let me look only to Jesus and live with a living faith like a mustard seed. I pray in the name of Jesus Christ. Amen.
