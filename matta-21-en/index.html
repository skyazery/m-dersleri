What will we learn today?

  • We will learn that Peter confessed Jesus as the 'Christ'.
  • We will learn that Jesus told beforehand that He would suffer for us.
  • We will answer the question, 'Who is Jesus to me?'
Scene 1 · Matthew 16:1–12

The disciples who forgot the bread

When the disciples crossed over to the other side in a boat, they forgot to take bread. When Jesus said, 'Beware of the of the Pharisees,' the disciples thought it was about bread.

Jesus reminded them of how He fed the five thousand and the four thousand. The yeast was the false teaching of the Pharisees. If we have Jesus, we don't need to worry about bread.

Scene 2 · Matthew 16:13–20

You are the Christ

Jesus asked, 'Who do people say I am?' The disciples answered, 'They say John the Baptist, Elijah, or Jeremiah.' 'But who do you say I am?'

Peter answered, 'You are the , the Son of the living God.' Jesus was happy: 'My Father in heaven revealed this to you. I will build My on this .'

Scene 3 · Matthew 16:21–23

Peter tried to stop Him

From that time, Jesus began to tell them that He would suffer in Jerusalem, be killed, and rise on the third day.

Peter took Jesus aside and said, 'May this never happen to You!' Jesus said, 'You are not thinking about the things of God, but the things of men.'

Follow Me
