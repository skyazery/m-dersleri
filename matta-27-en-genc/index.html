Bible lesson for teens · Matthew, lesson 27

Jesus Answers Questions

Matthew 21:23–22:46

Questions rain down in the temple in Jerusalem. The chief priests, Pharisees, Herodians, and Sadducees try to trip Jesus up one after another. Jesus navigates all the traps and finally gives the most essential answer: love.

Background

We understand what the three parables (the two sons, the wicked tenants, the wedding banquet) say about invitation and rejection in the kingdom of heaven.

Main idea

We can explain Jesus's wisdom in the question about paying taxes to Caesar and the greatest commandment.

Application

We make loving God and loving our neighbor a concrete priority in our lives.

1 · Matthew 21:23–32

Words and Actions

When the religious leaders ask, 'By what are you doing these things?' Jesus replies with a question about John's baptism. They couldn't answer.

Then comes the parable of the two sons. The younger son who said 'I will go' but didn't represents the outwardly religious leaders, while the older son who said 'I don't want to' but went represents the tax collectors and prostitutes. 'The tax collectors and the prostitutes are entering the kingdom of God ahead of you.'

Looking Deeper · The Wicked Tenants

In the parable in 21:33–46, the owner of the vineyard sends his servants, and finally his son. The tenants kill the son. Jesus foretells His own death and quotes Psalm 118:22: 'The stone which the builders rejected has become the cornerstone.'

2 · Matthew 22:1–14

Wedding Clothes

Those invited to the king's son's made excuses, and even killed the servants. The king says, 'Go to the street corners and invite to the banquet anyone you find.' Both bad and good were gathered at the banquet.

But one man was not wearing wedding clothes. The invitation is free, but one must respond worthily to that invitation. 'For many are called, but few are chosen.'

Looking Deeper · What are the wedding clothes?

There is an interpretation that in those days, the host of a banquet provided clothes for the guests. If so, not wearing the clothes was rejecting the host's gift. It means being invited by grace, but refusing to put on grace and insisting on one's own clothes (Isa. 61:10 'garments of salvation').

3 · Matthew 22:15–33

Whose Image Is It?

The Pharisees and Herodians came together. They were usually enemies, but they joined hands to trap Jesus. 'Is it right to pay taxes to Caesar?' If He said 'yes,' He would be a traitor to the people; if He said 'no,' He would be a rebel against Rome.

Jesus has them look at the '' and inscription on a denarius. 'Give to Caesar what is Caesar's, and to God what is God's.' If the emperor's image is on the coin, what is God's image engraved on? On us ourselves (Gen. 1:27).

Looking Deeper · The Resurrection Debate

In 22:23–33, the Sadducees, who don't believe in the resurrection, test Him with a story of a woman who married seven brothers. Jesus proves the resurrection using Exodus 3:6, saying, 'God is not the God of the dead, but of the living.'

4 · Matthew 22:34–46

The Foundation of the Entire Law

Rabbis counted the Law as 613 commandments. 'Which is the greatest commandment?' Jesus answers by combining Deuteronomy 6:5 (the Shema) and Leviticus 19:18: God and loving your neighbor.

'All the Law and the Prophets depend on these two commandments.' Like hinges holding a door, the whole Law hangs on these two. Then Jesus asks the final question: 'Whose son is the Christ?' He is the one David called 'Lord,' which is Jesus (Ps. 110:1).

Looking Deeper · The Two Are One

Jesus says the second commandment is 'like' the first. Whether love for God is genuine is shown by love for one's neighbor (1 John 4:20). Conversely, the power to love one's neighbor comes from loving God.

5 questions

Discussion questions

  1. Which of the two sons am I closer to? In what area are my words and actions different?
  2. What are my 'field' and 'business' that make me put off God's invitation?
  3. As God's image, what is it that I 'must give to God'?
  4. Between loving God and loving my neighbor, which one am I lacking more right now?

My Confession

Closing prayer

Dear God, thank You for inviting me to the banquet for free and clothing me with the righteousness of Jesus. Let me not just say 'yes' with my words, but obey. Let me give myself, who is Your image, to You, love You with all my heart, soul, and mind, and love my neighbor as myself. I pray in the name of Jesus Christ. Amen.
