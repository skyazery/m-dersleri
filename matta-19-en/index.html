Cheer up, it is I

What will we learn today?

  • We will learn that Jesus fed five thousand people with five loaves of bread and two fish.
  • We will learn that Jesus walked on the water.
  • We will learn to look at Jesus when we are afraid.
Scene 1 · Matthew 14:1–14

To a quiet place

Jesus heard the sad news that John the Baptist was killed because of King Herod. He got into a boat and went to a quiet place.

But many people followed Him on foot. When Jesus saw them, He on them and healed the sick.

Scene 2 · Matthew 14:15–21

Five loaves and two fish

When evening came, the disciples said, “Send the people away so they can go and buy food.” Jesus said, “You give them something to eat.” But there were only five loaves of and two fish.

Jesus looked up to heaven, , broke the bread, and gave it to them. There were five thousand men alone, everyone ate until they were full, and twelve baskets were left over!

Lord, save me

Peter said, “Lord, if it is You, command me to come to You on the water.” When Jesus said, “Come,” Peter also walked on the water!

But when he saw the wind, he was afraid and began to sink. “Lord, save me!” Jesus immediately reached out His hand and caught him. Those in the boat Him and said, “You are truly the Son of God!”
