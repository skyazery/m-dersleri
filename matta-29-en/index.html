Bible lesson for children · Matthew, lesson 29

To one of the least of these my brothers

Matthew 25:1–46

Tap the yellow words to see what they mean. The 🔊 button reads aloud.

What will we learn today?

  • We will decide to be wise people who are ready while waiting for Jesus.
  • We will learn to use the abilities God gave us well.
  • We will learn that helping someone in need is helping Jesus.
Scene 1 · Matthew 25:1–13

Ten girls and lamps

Ten girls took their and waited for the bridegroom. Five of them had prepared enough oil, but the other five had not.

When the bridegroom came late at night, the five girls who were ready went into the wedding, but the five who went to buy oil couldn't go in. We who are waiting for Jesus must always be ready too.

Scene 2 · Matthew 25:14–18

The talents entrusted by the master

A master going on a journey entrusted his servants with . He gave five to one, two to another, and one to another.

The servants who received five and two talents worked hard and doubled them. But the servant who received one talent dug a hole in the ground and hid the money.

Scene 3 · Matthew 25:19–30

Well done, good and faithful servant

The master returned and said to his hardworking servants, 'Well done, good and servant! You were faithful over a few things, I will put you in charge of many things.'

But the servant who hid the one talent said, 'I hid it because I was afraid of you.' The master scolded the lazy servant who didn't use what was entrusted to him. We shouldn't hide the abilities God gives us; we must use them.

Scene 4 · Matthew 25:31–46

You did it to me

On the day Jesus comes as King, He will say, 'I was hungry, and you gave me food; I was thirsty, and you gave me a drink; I was sick, and you cared for me.'

When people ask, 'When?', the King will answer, 'Whatever you did for one of the least of these of mine, you did it for me.' If we help a friend in need, we are helping Jesus.
