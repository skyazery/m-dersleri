The Cross

Matthew 27:1–66

Tap the yellow words to see what they mean. The 🔊 button reads aloud.

What will we learn today?

  • We will learn that Jesus was crucified even though He was sinless.
  • We will learn that Jesus carried the cross for me.
  • We will give thanks for the love of the cross.
Scene 1 · Matthew 27:11–26

Pilate and Barabbas

The Roman governor found no fault in Jesus. There was a custom to release a prisoner during the festival, so he asked, “Should I release Jesus, or the robber Barabbas?”

The crowd shouted, “Release Barabbas, and crucify Jesus!” To avoid responsibility, Pilate washed his hands and finally handed Jesus over. The sinful Barabbas went free, and the sinless Jesus died in his place.

Scene 2 · Matthew 27:27–32

Simon Carries the Cross

The soldiers put a crown of thorns on Jesus' head and mocked Him. Jesus was very tired, and it was hard for Him to carry the .

Truly the Son of God
