Bible lesson for children · Matthew, lesson 14

You are more valuable than sparrows

Matthew 10:1–42

Tap the yellow words to see what they mean. The 🔊 button reads aloud.

What will we learn today?

  • We will learn that Jesus chose and sent out twelve disciples.
  • We will learn that God cares for us and considers us more valuable than sparrows.
  • We will learn that we can serve Jesus even with a small act of kindness.
Scene 1 · Matthew 10:1–4

Twelve disciples

Jesus called his twelve and gave them power to heal the sick.

Scene 2 · Matthew 10:5–15

Sent out two by two

Jesus sent his disciples to the villages: “Announce that the kingdom of heaven is near, and heal the sick.”

“You received , freely give.” He told them not to take a lot of money or bags with them, but to trust only in God.

Scene 3 · Matthew 10:16–31

Don't be afraid

“I am sending you out as sheep among wolves.” Jesus told them in advance that there would be difficulties.

“Aren't two sold for a penny? But not one of them falls to the ground without your Father's permission. Even the very of your head are all numbered. Don't be afraid, you are more valuable than many sparrows.”

Scene 4 · Matthew 10:32–42

A cup of cold water

“Everyone who acknowledges me before people, I will also acknowledge before my Father in heaven.”

“Whoever gives one of my disciples a cup of will certainly not lose their reward.” Jesus remembers even small acts of kindness.
