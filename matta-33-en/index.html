He is risen!

Matthew 28:1–20

What will we learn today?

  • We will learn that Jesus rose on the third day.
  • We will learn the task the risen Jesus gave to His disciples.
  • We will believe that Jesus is always with us.
Scene 1 · Matthew 28:1–7

The empty tomb

Very early on Sunday morning, Mary Magdalene and the other Mary went to the tomb. Suddenly, there was a great earthquake. An came down, rolled away the stone, and sat on it.

The angel said, “Don't be afraid. Jesus is not here. He has , just as He said! Come and see the place where He lay.”

Scene 2 · Matthew 28:8–10

Greetings!

The women ran to tell the disciples, filled with both fear and great joy. Suddenly, Jesus met them and said, “Greetings!”

The women held Jesus' feet and worshiped Him. Jesus said, “Don't be afraid. Go and tell My brothers to go to Galilee. They will see Me there.”

Scene 3 · Matthew 28:11–15

Those who spread lies

The soldiers guarding the tomb went to the chief priests and told them what had happened. The chief priests gave the soldiers a lot of money and said:
