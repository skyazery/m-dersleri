Bible lesson for children · Matthew, lesson 8

Love Your Enemies

Matthew 5:17–48

Tap the yellow words to see what they mean. The 🔊 button reads aloud.

What will we learn today?

  • We will learn that Jesus came to fulfill the Word of God.
  • We will learn that it is important to reconcile first when we are angry.
  • We will learn to love and pray for the people who hurt us.
Scene 1 · Matthew 5:17–20

He came to fulfill the Word

Jesus said, “I didn't come to destroy God's , but to it.”

We need to obey God's Word from the heart, not just on the outside.

Scene 2 · Matthew 5:21–26

Reconcile first

“You were told, ‘Don't murder.’ But I tell you: being angry with your brother in your heart is also a sin.”

“If you remember that your friend is upset with you when you bring a gift to God, go and with them first, then come back.”

Scene 3 · Matthew 5:38–42

Go two miles with him

At that time, a Roman soldier could force people to carry his load for one mile.

Jesus said, “Whoever forces you to go one mile, go with him two miles.” We should answer bad things with , not with more bad things.

Scene 4 · Matthew 5:43–48

Love your enemies

“Love your and for those who persecute you.”

God makes His sun rise on both the good and the bad, and sends rain on everyone. We also become children who are like our Father in heaven.
