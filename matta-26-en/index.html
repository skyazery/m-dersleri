Bible lesson for children · Matthew, lesson 26

Hosanna!

Matthew 21:1–22

Tap the yellow words to see what they mean. The 🔊 button reads aloud.

What will we learn today?

  • We will learn that Jesus rode a donkey into Jerusalem.
  • We will learn that the temple should be a house of prayer.
  • We will praise Jesus just like the children did.
Scene 1 · Matthew 21:1–11

The King on a Donkey

Jesus didn't ride a fancy horse, but a little into Jerusalem. He is a humble King.

People spread their coats and tree branches on the road and shouted, “ to the Son of David! Blessed is He who comes in the name of the Lord!”

Scene 2 · Matthew 21:12–13

A House of Prayer

Jesus went into the , but instead of praying, people were buying, selling, and making a lot of noise.

Jesus overturned the tables and said, “My house will be called a house of prayer, but you are making it a den of robbers!”

Scene 3 · Matthew 21:14–17

The Praise of Children

In the temple, the blind and the lame came to Jesus, and He healed them. The children shouted with joy, “Hosanna to the Son of David!”

The chief priests were angry, but Jesus said, “Have you never read, ‘Out of the mouth of children and nursing babies you have prepared ’?” God is pleased with the praise of children.

Scene 4 · Matthew 21:18–22

The Fig Tree Without Fruit

In the morning, Jesus was hungry. He went up to a , but it only had leaves and not a single fruit. As soon as Jesus spoke to it, the tree dried up.

Jesus said, “You shouldn't just look good on the outside without having any fruit. If you pray with faith, you will receive whatever you ask for.”
