Bible lesson for teens · Matthew, lesson 14

The Sent Ones

Matthew 10:1–42

At the end of chapter 9, Jesus said, 'Pray for workers to be sent out.' In chapter 10, He Himself sends out those same disciples who prayed. You yourself might be the answer to that prayer.

Background

We understand the composition of the twelve apostles and the meaning of their sending.

Main idea

We can explain the words of comfort given in the midst of persecution and fear.

Application

We identify concrete ways to acknowledge Jesus in our lives.

1 · Matthew 10:1–4

The twelve and the names

The number twelve brings to mind the twelve tribes of Israel. Jesus is building God's new people. A 'disciple' (learner) now becomes an '' (sent one).

The list is surprising. Matthew, who collected taxes for Rome, is together with Simon the Zealot, who fought against Rome. And at the end is Judas, who would betray Him.

A Deeper Look · Zealots and tax collectors

Zealots were people who wanted to drive Rome out by force of arms. Tax collectors collaborated with that same Rome. In Jesus, two people who were enemies became one community.

2 · Matthew 10:5–15

You received freely

Jesus first tells them to go to 'the lost sheep of the house of Israel.' Later in chapter 28, this expands to 'all nations.' God's plan has an order.

The disciples are sent to do what Jesus did (preach, heal). He tells them not to put money in their belts and to stay in a house that welcomes them. 'You received , freely give.'

A Deeper Look · Wish them peace

When entering a house, they should wish it 'shalom' (peace). If they don't welcome it, they should shake the dust off their feet. We leave the results to God, while the disciple must faithfully proclaim.

3 · Matthew 10:16–31

Sheep among wolves

“Be wise as serpents, and innocent as doves.” Jesus does not hide that the disciples will be taken to court and hated even by their families. The Holy Spirit will give them the words to say at that time.

“Don't be afraid of those who kill the body but are not able to kill the soul.” Then He gives the example of two sparrows sold for an . If God remembers even a sparrow, “you are more valuable than many sparrows.”

A Deeper Look · The direction of fear

In 10:26–31, 'don't be afraid' appears three times. The secret to not fearing people is to properly fear God. A person who knows God is free before people.

4 · Matthew 10:32–42

Acknowledging and a cup of cold water

“Everyone who me before people, I will also acknowledge before my Father in heaven.” To follow Jesus is to carry a cross.

But the final words are warm. Whoever welcomes a disciple welcomes Jesus, and whoever gives one of these little ones a cup of cold water will not lose their reward. Not only great martyrdom but also small hospitality is the work of the kingdom of heaven.

A Deeper Look · Whoever loses their life will find it

“Whoever finds their life will lose it, and whoever loses their life for my sake will find it” (10:39). This is the first time the word 'cross' appears in the Gospel of Matthew. Jesus Himself will go that way first.

Discussion questions

  1. Have I ever prayed 'send workers' but didn't want to go myself?

My confession

Closing prayer

Jesus, just as You called twelve ordinary and different people, made them one, and sent them out, send me out too. Let me not fear people, but fear God. Even if it's not a great work, let me live acknowledging You through kindness like a cup of cold water. I pray in the name of Jesus Christ. Amen.
