Bible lesson for children · Matthew, lesson 23

A Lost Sheep

Matthew 18:1–35

Tap the yellow words to see what they mean. The 🔊 button reads aloud.

What will we learn today?

  • We will learn that the greatest in the kingdom of heaven is someone who is humble like a child.
  • We will learn that God looks for us just as a shepherd looks for a lost sheep.
  • We will learn to forgive our friends just as God forgave us.
Scene 1 · Matthew 18:1–5

Who is the greatest?

The disciples asked, “Who is the greatest in the kingdom of heaven?” Jesus called a little child and had him stand among them.

“Unless you change and become like little children, you will never enter the kingdom of heaven. Therefore, whoever humbles himself like this child is the in the kingdom of heaven.”

Scene 2 · Matthew 18:10–14

The shepherd looking for the lost sheep

“If a has a hundred sheep, and one of them wanders away, he leaves the ninety-nine and goes to look for the one that wandered off.”

“And if he finds it, he is happier about that one sheep than about the ninety-nine that did not wander off. In the same way, your Father in heaven does not want any of these little ones to be .”

Scene 3 · Matthew 18:21–22

Seven times?

Peter asked, “Lord, how many times should I my friend when he sins against me? Up to seven times?”

Jesus answered, “I tell you, not seven times, but seventy-seven times.” This means to forgive without counting.

Scene 4 · Matthew 18:23–35

The unforgiving servant

A servant owed the king a huge he could not pay. When the servant begged, the king took pity on him and canceled the whole debt.

But that servant went out, grabbed his fellow servant who owed him very little, and threw him into prison. The king was angry: “Shouldn't you have had mercy on your fellow servant just as I had on you?”
