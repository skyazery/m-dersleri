The Last Supper

What will we learn today?

  • We will learn that the woman who poured perfume gave Jesus her most precious thing.
  • We will learn the meaning of Jesus giving bread and a cup at the Last Supper.
  • We will decide to give our best to Jesus.
Scene 1 · Matthew 26:6–13

The Woman Who Poured Precious Perfume

In Bethany, a woman brought very expensive and poured it on Jesus' head. The whole room was filled with a beautiful fragrance.

The disciples said, "Why is she wasting it?" but Jesus said, "This woman did a good thing for Me. She prepared Me for burial. What this woman did will be told all over the world."

Scene 2 · Matthew 26:14–16

Jesus Sold for Thirty Pieces of Silver

But one of the disciples, Iscariot, went to the chief priests and said, "What will you give me if I hand Jesus over to you?"

They gave him thirty pieces of silver. The woman gave Jesus her most precious thing, but Judas traded Jesus for money.

Scene 3 · Matthew 26:17–30

This is My Body, My Blood

On the evening of the , Jesus ate with His disciples. Jesus broke the bread, gave it to them, and said, "Take, eat; this is My body."

Then He gave them the cup and said, "This is My blood of the . It is poured out for many for the forgiveness of sins." Jesus was going to give His body and blood for us.

Scene 4 · Matthew 26:31–35

Before the Rooster Crows

On the way to the Mount of Olives, Jesus said, "Tonight, all of you will leave Me."

When Peter said, "I will never leave You!" Jesus said, "Tonight, before the crows, you will deny Me three times." Even so, Jesus loved His disciples to the very end.
