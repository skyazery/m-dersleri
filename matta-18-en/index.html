Treasure Hidden in a Field

Matthew 13:24–58

Tap the yellow words to see what they mean. The 🔊 button reads aloud.

What will we learn today?

  • We will learn various parables explaining the kingdom of heaven.
  • We will learn that the kingdom of heaven starts small and grows big.
  • We will confess that Jesus is a treasure more valuable than anything in the world.
Scene 1 · Matthew 13:24–30

Wheat and weeds

A man sowed good wheat seed in his field. But at night, his enemy came and sowed seeds and went away.

When his servants wanted to pull up the weeds, the owner said, “The wheat might be uprooted too, let them grow together until the harvest.” God will separate them correctly on the last day.

Scene 2 · Matthew 13:31–33

Mustard seed and yeast

Treasure and pearl

“When a merchant looking for good found a very valuable pearl, he sold all he had and bought it.” Jesus is this valuable.

A net gathering fish

“The kingdom of heaven is like a thrown into the sea that gathers all kinds of fish. Fishermen put the good fish into baskets and throw the bad ones away.”

Jesus asked, “Have you understood all these things?” The disciples answered, “Yes!”
