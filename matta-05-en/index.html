Bible lesson for children · Matthew, lesson 5

Jesus Overcomes with the Word

Matthew 4:1–11

Tap the yellow words to see what they mean. The 🔊 button reads aloud.

What will we learn today?

  • We will learn that Jesus was tempted in the wilderness.
  • We will learn that Jesus overcame temptation with God's Word.
  • We will learn to remember the Word and say 'no' when temptation comes.
Scene 1 · Matthew 4:1–2

40 Days in the Wilderness

After being baptized, the Holy Spirit led Jesus into the .

Jesus and prayed for 40 days without eating anything. Then He was very hungry.

Scene 2 · Matthew 4:3–4

Turn Stones into Bread

Then the came and Jesus: “If You are the Son of God, tell these stones to become bread.”

Jesus answered with words from the Bible: “Man shall not live by bread alone, but by every that comes from the mouth of God.”

Scene 3 · Matthew 4:5–10

Jump Down, Worship Me

The devil took Jesus to the roof of the and said, “Throw Yourself down, the angels will catch You.” Jesus overcame by saying, “Do not test your God.”

The devil showed Him all the kingdoms of the world from a high mountain and said, “If You worship me, I will give You all of these.” Jesus said, “Go away, Satan! only God.”

Scene 4 · Matthew 4:11

Angels Served Him

Finally, the devil left Jesus alone and went away. Jesus won!

Then came and served Jesus. God's Word is the strongest power to overcome temptation.
